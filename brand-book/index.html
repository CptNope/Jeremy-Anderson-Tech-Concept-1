01

Positioning

What Jeremy Anderson Tech is, who it is for, and the claim no neighbor can truthfully copy.

For organizations that run on their website and software, Jeremy Anderson Tech is the engineering studio that designs, builds, secures and runs the whole system, so one accountable expert owns the outcome.

Positioning statement

For
Small and mid-sized businesses in Central Massachusetts and Greater Boston, plus research organizations and funded teams anywhere, whose website, tools and data have become operational infrastructure.
Who need
Systems that work on day one and keep working: secure, accessible, fast, found in search and AI answers, and understood by the people who pay for them.
JAT is
A founder-led engineering studio covering web platforms, applied AI, cybersecurity and infrastructure under one roof.
Unlike
Web shops that hand off at launch, large agencies that route you through account managers, and IT providers who do not build software.
Because
The person who architects the system also hardens it, hosts it and answers when it needs attention, at prices published before the first call.

Strategic differentiation

OneAccountable lead

One engineer spans web, AI, security and hosting. No hand-offs between vendors, no blame gaps.

DefaultSecure and accessible

Hardening and WCAG 2.2 AA are part of every build, backed by W3C/WAI accessibility certification, never upsold.

OpenPublished pricing

Fixed-fee packages and hourly rates are on the website. Scope changes go through written change orders.

RunBuilt to be operated

Managed hosting, monitoring and care plans continue the work after launch on infrastructure JAT runs.

PublicProof you can inspect

Open-source projects with live demos and public code, so buyers judge the engineering directly.

Current state and the move

Today on jeremyanderson.tech

A freelancer portfolio. The hero leads with a personal name and the label "Software Architect" while the page title says "Full-Stack Developer & WordPress Engineer". Skills are grouped by technology. Services, prices and process are strong but buried two clicks deep.

The move

An agency presentation that leads with the outcome, organizes everything into four service channels, puts pricing and process in plain view, and lets the open-source work prove the engineering. Jeremy stays visible as the founder and the person you will work with.

Jeremy Anderson Tech is a branded house. Services are grouped into four channels, named the way an oscilloscope names its inputs. Each channel owns one color, used only as an identifier. The channels describe what the client gets; the services underneath describe how.

Messaging rules

Do
  • Lead with the outcome, name the technology second.
  • Use numbers that can be verified, or none.
  • State prices and timelines plainly.
  • Write short sentences in active voice.
  • Say "we" for the studio and "Jeremy" when it is personally him.
  • Explain one technical idea per paragraph.
Don't
  • Invent clients, quotes, metrics or team members.
  • Use fear to sell security.
  • Lead with a stack list.
  • Promise "100%", "bulletproof" or "guaranteed rankings".
  • Use "cutting-edge", "synergy", "revolutionary", "AI-powered" as filler.
  • Use hacker, hoodie or brain imagery in words or pictures.

08

Logo system

A monogram drawn as one beam trace, a wordmark set in outlined Archivo Expanded, and a ruler that measures.

Primary horizontal lockup on Graticule. The JAT trace reads as a step response: the line enters at rest, the J and A form the transient, and the T settles at the trigger level.

Exploration

Construction

Grid
16-unit divisions on a 108 × 68 artboard (viewBox 1 6 108 68).
Stroke
8 units, round caps, round joins. Half of one division.
Levels
Trigger level y 16. Baseline y 64. The J tail starts at y 50.
Apex
The A peaks exactly on the trigger level at x 52; the T bar starts 18 units later.
Unit X
One division, 16 units. X sets clear space and lockup spacing.

Primary and secondary logos

Color variants

Clear space and minimum size

Keep at least X clear on every side, where X is one grid division: the height of the J tail, or about 0.29 × the mark's height. Never place other marks, text or edges inside that zone.

Production rules

  • The wordmark is outlined from Archivo Expanded (width 125, weight 620, tracking +40). TECH uses weight 380, tracking +320. No live text inside logo files.
  • The monogram stays a stroke in source files so weight can be audited. Expand strokes only for embroidery, vinyl cutting and laser etching.
  • Recolor with currentColor or the --mk, --lk-name, --lk-tech, --lk-rule custom properties. Never edit path data.
  • Favicons: export jat-icon.svg at 16, 32, 180, 192 and 512 px; ship the SVG as rel="icon" for modern browsers.
  • Print: convert to CMYK at the printer using the values in section 10. Spot alternative: Pantone 319 C for Channel Cyan.

Pairing rules

  • Headings run expanded (112–125); body text stays at width 100 for reading comfort.
  • Emphasis comes from weight and width, never italics, color gradients or underlines.
  • Mono appears only when the content is data. A headline in mono is a costume.
  • Running text measure: 60–72 characters. Headings wrap with text-wrap: balance.

Show real work and real people. Draw systems as signals. Move like an instrument settling.

Photography

Photography documents. It shows Jeremy working with clients, real screens of shipped projects, and the places in Central Massachusetts where the work happens. It never illustrates abstract ideas like "security" or "AI".

Natural daylight, side-lit, cool neutral white balance.

Motion

Run
Triggered
Things arrive unsettled and lock into place, then stop. One trigger sequence per page, on the hero.
Measured

15

Accessibility and responsive rules

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor. The founder holds W3C/WAI accessibility certification, so the brand must visibly live up to it.

Contrast

Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text and UI boundaries ≥ 3:1. Use the matrix in section 10. Channel colors never carry body text on paper.

Focus

2px Cyan outline with 2px offset on every focusable element. Never remove focus styles. Skip link first in the tab order.

Reduced motion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion: static locked trace, no sweeps, no smooth scroll, no parallax. Motion is never required to understand content.

Not color alone

Channels always show their number and name. States pair color with an icon and text.

Targets and type

44 × 44px minimum targets. Body text at least 16px. Text resizes to 200% without loss.

Canvas

Decorative canvases are aria-hidden; every diagram has a text alternative. Canvases pause when off screen.
