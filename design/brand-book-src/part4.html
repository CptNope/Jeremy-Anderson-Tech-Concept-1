Show real work and real people. Draw systems as signals. Move like an instrument settling.

Photography

Photography documents. It shows Jeremy working with clients, real screens of shipped projects, and the places in Central Massachusetts where the work happens. It never illustrates abstract ideas like "security" or "AI".

Light
Natural daylight, side-lit, cool neutral white balance.
Grade
Shadows lifted toward Ink 900, saturation −15%, skin tones untouched. No color casts in channel hues.
Framing
Generous negative space on one side so graticule overlays and readouts can sit beside, never on, faces.
Screens
Capture real browser windows at 2× and place them in plain device frames. No fake code, no lens flares.

Illustration: trace diagrams

Systems are drawn as signal paths on a graticule. Single 1.5px strokes, channel color by discipline, labels in Martian Mono. Every diagram must describe something real.

Iconography

Authored on a 24px grid, 1.5px stroke, round caps and joins, 2px interior padding. Icons share the mark's stroke logic. They label; they never decorate headings.

Motion

Run
Triggered
Things arrive unsettled and lock into place, then stop. One trigger sequence per page, on the hero.
Measured
Durations come from tokens: 120 / 200 / 320 / 640 ms. Exponential ease-out cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1).
Quiet at rest
After a sequence completes, the canvas stops drawing. Nothing loops in the background.
Answers the pointer
Measurement cursors follow the pointer on screens; hover states shift by 3px or a border color, never bounce.
Reduced motion
Render the locked final frame immediately. Remove sweeps and smooth scrolling.

Interaction principles

  • Controls name their action: "Book a discovery call", never "Submit" or "Learn more".
  • Hover reveals measurement, not decoration: borders turn Cyan, arrows advance 3px.
  • Every interactive element has a 44px minimum target and a visible Cyan focus ring.
  • Errors state what is wrong and how to fix it, next to the field.
  • No modals for tasks that do not need interruption. Forms live in the page.

Accessibility and responsive rules

WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor. The founder holds W3C/WAI accessibility certification, so the brand must visibly live up to it.

Contrast

Body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text and UI boundaries ≥ 3:1. Use the matrix in section 10. Channel colors never carry body text on paper.

Focus

2px Cyan outline with 2px offset on every focusable element. Never remove focus styles. Skip link first in the tab order.

Reduced motion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion: static locked trace, no sweeps, no smooth scroll, no parallax. Motion is never required to understand content.

Not color alone

Channels always show their number and name. States pair color with an icon and text.

Targets and type

44 × 44px minimum targets. Body text at least 16px. Text resizes to 200% without loss.

Canvas

Decorative canvases are aria-hidden; every diagram has a text alternative. Canvases pause when off screen.
