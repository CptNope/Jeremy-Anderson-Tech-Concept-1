God's Eye Lite

A free 3D world map that combines live public data with outdoor navigation, and runs almost entirely in the browser.

CH1 PlatformsCH4 Operations

Schematic illustration of the interface. Open the live app for the real thing.

The brief

Make a world of live public data usable by anyone, on any device, without an account and without a server bill that grows with every visitor.

What it does

  • Shows orbiting satellites and their paths, with orbital positions calculated in the browser.
  • Layers recent earthquakes, upcoming launches, radar, clouds, wind, cyclone tracks, lightning and warnings.
  • Aggregates thousands of public traffic cameras from transportation agencies and municipalities.
  • Adds trails, topographic maps, peaks, water sources, campsites and current conditions for the outdoors.
  • Records GPS tracks on the device and exports them as GPX. Track data never leaves the phone.
  • Caches chosen map areas for offline use.

Engineering decisions

Static first. The app is served as a static site from GitHub Pages, and the browser fetches most data directly from public services. No database, no app server.

Pre-built catalogs. Agencies block browsers from downloading full camera catalogs, so a scheduled GitHub Actions job collects and processes camera metadata and publishes it with the app.

A thin edge layer. Cloudflare Workers fill only the gaps browser restrictions create, instead of standing up a traditional backend.

Keys stay home. Optional third-party API keys live in an encrypted vault on the user's device and unlock temporarily, never on a central server.

What it means for clients

The same approach keeps business systems fast and cheap to run: push work to the edge, cache aggressively, keep sensitive data on the device or in your own infrastructure, and automate the chores with scheduled jobs.
