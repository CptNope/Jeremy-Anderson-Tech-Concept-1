# Changelog

## 1.0.0 · 2026-10-10

First release.

- **Drop-in replacement for Developer Portfolio.** It keeps all 22 `developer-portfolio/*` blocks, with the same names, attributes and defaults. It also keeps the `project` and `service` post types, their taxonomies and meta, the page templates, the preset slugs, the patterns, the `[faq]` shortcode and the `{year}` token.
- **Calibrated Signal design** for every template:
  - front page, pages, posts, the blog;
  - projects and services, single and archive;
  - taxonomies, search and 404.
- **14 `jat/*` blocks** for the concept layouts. Every block is server-rendered and has a live preview and a settings sidebar in the editor.
- **Appearance › JAT Signal:**
  - apply the concept layout to Home, Services, About and Projects;
  - restore the previous layout with one click;
  - create the concept pages;
  - set the contact email.
- **Activation on an existing site** adds the Approach, Contact and four channel pages as drafts and changes nothing else.
- **Activation on a new install** sets up the full site:
  - the 27 services and their prices;
  - Home, Services, Projects, About and Blog, with the concept layouts applied;
  - the concept pages, published;
  - the legal pages and the tech stack.
- **Contact form** with validation, a honeypot, a minimum fill time and a rate limit. Every message is emailed and also stored under Inquiries. It can show a Contact Form 7 form instead.
- **SEO.** Meta tags and structured data, which step aside for All in One SEO, Yoast, Rank Math, SEOPress and The SEO Framework.
- **Fonts.** Archivo and Martian Mono are self-hosted variable fonts.
